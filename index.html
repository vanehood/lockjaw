Description

A reserve token on Robinhood Chain with OHM mechanisms built in. Staking, bonding, and a treasury backed floor, with supply rebasing every eight hours. Stake once and the balance grows on its own.

Assembly notes
ITEM 1

Stake

Deposit JAW and receive sJAW one for one. Every eight hours the contract raises every sJAW balance by the rebase rate. Nothing to claim, nothing to harvest. The number in the wallet changes on its own.

Unstake at any time and receive JAW back at the current ratio.

ITEM 2

Bond

Sell liquidity or a reserve asset to the treasury, receive JAW at a discount, vested linearly across five days. Bonding is how the treasury takes assets on, and the discount is what pays for the lockup.

Bond pricing tracks demand. Heavy bonding compresses the discount, quiet periods widen it.

ITEM 3

Treasury

Everything bonded stays in the treasury and backs the supply. Each JAW is nominally backed by a fraction of what is held, which sets a floor rather than a price.

Market price trades independently of backing, above it and below it.

General notes / read before staking
  1. The APY is supply expansion, not income. It comes from the protocol printing tokens and dividing them among stakers. It is not interest, fees, or revenue from outside the system. If supply grows faster than demand, price per token falls while the balance rises.
  2. Rebasing protects nobody on its own. Holding a growing count of tokens only pays if the token holds value. Track the position in what was deposited, not in token count.
  3. Every protocol built on this mechanism has been highly volatile. Most fell sharply once new deposits slowed. Treat that as the expected pattern, size accordingly, and commit only what can be lost in full.
