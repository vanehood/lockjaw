01 THE THREE MECHANISMS

What the protocol actually does

Three parts, all inherited from the OHM design. Nothing here is reinvented. It runs on Robinhood Chain, where the mechanism has not run before.

01

Stake

Deposit JAW, receive sJAW one for one. Every eight hours the contract raises every sJAW balance by the rebase rate.

Unstake whenever you want and get JAW back at the current ratio.

NOTHING TO CLAIM
02

Bond

Sell liquidity or a reserve asset to the treasury and get JAW at a discount, vested linearly over five days.

Heavy bonding compresses the discount, quiet periods widen it.

5 DAY LINEAR VEST
03

Treasury

Everything bonded stays in the treasury and backs the supply. Each JAW is nominally backed by a fraction of what is held.

That sets a floor, not a price. The market trades above it and below it.

BACKING IS A FLOOR
READ THIS BEFORE YOU STAKE

The APY is supply expansion, not income. It comes from the protocol printing tokens and splitting them among stakers. It is not interest, fees, or revenue from outside the system. If supply grows faster than demand, the price per token falls while your balance rises.

Rebasing protects nobody on its own. Holding a growing count of tokens only pays if the token holds value. Track your position in what you deposited, not in token count.

Every protocol built on this mechanism has been highly volatile. Most fell hard once new deposits slowed. Treat that as the expected pattern, size accordingly, and only commit what you can lose in full.
